Services

We work with all types of Latvian public procurement in the Electronic Procurement System (EIS): supplies, services and works.

1

ESPD and qualification documents

We fill in the ESPD (EVIPD in Latvian) from the tender requirements and prepare the qualification document package.

  • Compliance check against the tender regulations
  • List of past projects and references
  • Check of key experts, certificates and subcontractors
2

Full bid

On top of qualification, we prepare a draft technical proposal and check the financial proposal form.

  • Technical proposal following the structure of the tender regulations
  • Arithmetic and format check of the financial form (you set the price)
  • Document checklist and timeline
3

Tender assessment

Before starting, we read the tender regulations and tell you honestly whether you have a real chance and what you will need to obtain.

  • Qualification requirements and missing documents
  • Deadlines: questions to the contracting authority, site visit, submission
  • Award criteria (price only, or price + quality)
What we don't do: we don't submit or sign bids on your behalf, we don't keep your EIS or e-signature credentials, we don't prepare complaints to the Procurement Monitoring Bureau (IUB) or represent you in court (for those, we recommend a lawyer), and we don't work for contracting authorities.

How it works

The sooner you send us a tender, the more time is left for questions to the contracting authority. We recommend at least 10 working days before the submission deadline.

  1. Enter your company nameFrom the name or registration number, we work out your sector and past tenders ourselves and find suitable ones for you in EIS. If you have already found a tender, add its link too.
  2. We assessWe check the qualification requirements, deadlines and whether we already work with another bidder in this tender (lot). We tell you whether and how we can help, and at what price.
  3. We agreeA written contract setting out the scope, price and the date you will receive the documents.
  4. You send us the informationReferences, CVs, certificates, financial data. Your company is responsible for their accuracy.
  5. We prepare the documentsThe ESPD, qualification package and, if ordered, the technical proposal. A person checks everything before handover.
  6. You review and submitYou set the price, sign with your own e-signature and submit from your own EIS account and your own device.

C · mixed

Small upfront fee + low %

79–99 € upfront

+ 2 % of the contract value if you sign a contract with the contracting authority.

How the success fee is calculated (A and C): the contract value is the contract price excluding VAT in the contract you sign with the contracting authority (your share). For framework agreements and multi-year contracts, it is the value for the first 12 months or, if that cannot be determined, the total actually invoiced in the first 12 months. The success fee is payable within 14 days of signing the contract with the contracting authority. The fixed price (B) and upfront fee (C) are payable within 14 days of document handover.

The success fee is payment for document preparation only. We do not contact the contracting authority or the evaluation committee on your behalf, and we do not guarantee a win. Prices exclude VAT.

Frequently asked questions

Who submits the bid in EIS?

You do. Your company signs and submits the bid from its own EIS account and its own device. If we need to work in EIS, your administrator can create a user for us with the role "Supplier bid preparer" (Piegādātāja piedāvājuma sagatavotājs), which you block after the tender. Do not give us your e-signature tools or passwords.

Do you also work with my competitors?

In each tender (or tender lot) we work with only one bidder. We never share or use other clients' prices, documents or solutions. Each client has a separate workspace. This reduces the risk of prohibited agreements (Section 11 of the Competition Law) for both you and us.

What does "first job free" mean?

For the first tender (or one lot) your company entrusts to us, preparation is free, even if you win. This applies once per company.

Do you guarantee a win?

No. We help you submit a complete bid that meets the requirements, but the contracting authority chooses the winner based on the criteria in the tender regulations.

How early should we get in touch?

We recommend at least 10 working days before the submission deadline, to leave time for questions to the contracting authority and for collecting missing documents. With shorter deadlines we help where it's realistic, and we tell you honestly before starting.

Small procurements don't require an ESPD. Can you help?

Yes. In small procurements (Section 9 of the Public Procurement Law) an ESPD is usually not required, so we prepare the qualification documents and application according to the tender regulations.

Do you use artificial intelligence?

Yes, we use software tools, including artificial intelligence, to prepare documents. A person checks every document before handover, and your data is not used in work for other clients.

What if we lost and want to file a complaint?

We don't prepare complaints to the Procurement Monitoring Bureau (IUB) or court applications. We recommend contacting a lawyer; we can pass on the facts and documents from our work.

Do you help with tenders abroad?

We currently work with Latvian tenders in EIS. If you are interested in a specific tender in another EU country, write to us and we'll tell you whether we can help.

What is your liability?

We prepare documents carefully, but the client reviews and signs the ESPD and the bid, and is responsible for the truthfulness of the information provided and for submitting the bid. Our liability is limited to the fee paid for the specific bid.
